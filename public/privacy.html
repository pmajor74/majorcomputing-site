Privacy PolicyPolitique de confidentialitéPolítica de privacidad · 30 August 2026

The short version. The game collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server: everything it remembers is stored on your own device. The only component that can send anything anywhere is advertising, which stops for good once it is removed — with one small purchase, or by watching your way to it — and which asks for your consent first wherever the law requires it.

Who this is from

Colored Sands of Egypt is published by Major Computing, in British Columbia, Canada. Questions about anything on this page can go to admin@majorcomputing.ca.

What the game stores, and where

Your progress through the chambers, your best results, your settings — sound, music, vibration, colour-blind symbols — and whether you have removed advertising are all kept in the app's own storage on your device. None of it is sent to us. We never see it, and we could not retrieve it if we wanted to. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The game is fully playable with no network connection, and nothing in it waits on one.

Advertising

The game can show occasional full-screen advertisements between puzzles, supplied by Google AdMob. It never interrupts a puzzle in progress, and it is never attached to undo or restart — those are free and unlimited for everyone, always. Separately, when a hint or the extra vial has run out, you may choose to watch an advert for one instead of waiting a minute; that is your choice each time, and the wait is always there.

When advertising is switched on, Google's Mobile Ads SDK operates inside the app and may collect information of its own, typically including a device advertising identifier, coarse location inferred from your IP address, and basic usage signals, in order to select and measure adverts. That collection is Google's, governed by Google's own privacy terms; we receive none of it beyond aggregate counts of how many adverts were shown.

The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it carries no code to do so.

You can remove advertising permanently, either with a single small purchase or by choosing to watch adverts until the same amount has been earned. There is no subscription, and nothing recurring.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card, your billing address or your account. The app is told only whether the one product has been bought, and it stores that answer on your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children in particular. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect personal information at all.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct or erase personal data apply to data a company holds. We hold none. If you want the game to forget everything it knows about your play, delete the app; that is the whole of it.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever begins collecting something, this page will say so before it does, and the date at the top will change. There is no version of this document that quietly grows.
