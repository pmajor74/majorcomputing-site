Colored Sands of Egypt

A calm sand-sorting puzzle.Un casse-tête de tri de sable, tout en calme.Un sereno rompecabezas de arena.

Each chamber hands you vessels of layered coloured sand. Pour one into another until every pigment is gathered into a vessel of its own. Sand only pours onto sand of the same colour, and only where there is room for it.

Undo and restart are free and unlimited, for everyone, forever. They are not sold, they are not rationed, and there is no energy bar and no way to lose. Hints and an extra vial come three at a time, come back for nothing after a minute's wait or for an advert you choose to watch, or are yours unlimited for one small payment. One small payment removes the advertising, or you can earn the same thing by watching adverts until it is paid for. That is the whole of the money in this game, and nothing in it forces you to pay.

Help

A vessel with a narrow neck
Takes one layer per pour, however much you tip into it.
A vessel behind a stone grille
Pours out, but nothing can be poured in.
I am stuck
The Hint button is in the toolbar at the bottom of a chamber: three free ones, then another every minute you wait, for an advert you choose to watch, or unlimited once. Undo is beside it, free, and goes back as far as you like.
I cannot tell two colours apart
Turn on Symbols under Options. Every pigment gets its own hieroglyph.
I paid, and the adverts came back
Options → Restore purchases. It is the same purchase on every device signed in to your store account, at no further cost.
Does it work without internet?
Entirely. The whole game is on the device and nothing waits on a network.
